Hello! My name is Marco Sandoval and I am from Arizona, USA. I like to draw realism, study scriptures, and spend time with my family.

Arizona

Arizona is a wonderful place, expecially the mid northern areas. From within Maricopa County, you can see mountains surrounding the outskirts of the civilised parts of the desert. The mid-northern area contains a variety of beautiful green forestry which is a great difference compared to the dry desert just south of it. It is incredibly hot ten months our of the year, and the winters here can sometimes only be noticed between sunrise and sunset.
